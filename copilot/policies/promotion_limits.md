# Promotion Limits Policy

Promotional discounts are capped by category to protect the reference
price customers expect to see.

Soft drinks and snacks: promotions may discount up to 20% off list price
without additional approval.

Cereal and dairy: promotions may discount up to 15% off list price
without additional approval.

Premium beverages: promotions are capped at 10% off list price. Deeper
discounts require director approval, since promotions in this category
have historically eroded willingness to pay at full price for 6-8 weeks
afterward.

No more than one promotion per product may run in any rolling 4-week
window, to avoid training customers to wait for the next discount.
