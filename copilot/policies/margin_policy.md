# Minimum Margin Policy

Every price recommendation must respect the minimum contribution margin
for its category. These floors exist independently of whatever margin
constraint a requester types in an ad-hoc query -- a looser request never
overrides company policy.

Soft drinks: minimum margin of 25%. This is a high-velocity, price-sensitive
category; margin is protected mainly through volume.

Snacks: minimum margin of 28%. Moderate price sensitivity, frequent
promotional activity.

Cereal: minimum margin of 30%. Private-label competition keeps this
category under constant pricing pressure.

Dairy: minimum margin of 32%. Shorter shelf life increases the cost of
being wrong on the low side.

Premium beverages: minimum margin of 35%. This category is priced on
brand and status positioning, not on unit economics -- discounting it
signals desperation more than value, and depresses the category's
long-term price ceiling.

Any recommendation below the category floor requires category-manager
sign-off and must be logged with a written justification.
