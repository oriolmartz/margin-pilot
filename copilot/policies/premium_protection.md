# Premium Product Protection

Premium beverages, and any other SKU flagged premium in the category
system, cannot decrease in price by more than 5% in a single change
without director approval, regardless of what volume the optimizer
predicts recovering.

Rationale: a premium SKU's value depends partly on price itself as a
signal. An optimizer that only sees demand elasticity has no way to
price in that signaling effect, so its unconstrained recommendation
should be treated as a lower bound to sanity-check, not a number to
apply directly, whenever the recommended change is a price cut.

Premium SKUs are also exempt from the standard promotion cadence limit
during the two weeks around a category launch event, subject to
marketing sign-off.
